The Clock That Forgot Time

Amelia Rowan

                                             The Clock That Forgot Time When Time Stopped, the Mystery Began By Amelia Rowan

At exactly midnight, every clock in the town stopped. Every clock except one.

It belonged to fourteen-year-old Leo.
He found it in an old box in his grandfather’s attic. It was small, silver, and had no numbers on its face.Instead, a single word was engraved beneath its hands: REMEMBER.
The strange thing was that the clock never showed the same time twice.

One evening, Leo noticed something even stranger.
The clock had stopped.
Its hands pointed toward a tiny symbol on the face—a door. Leo followed the symbol to an abandoned railway station outside town. Behind the station, hidden beneath a pile of ivy, was a blue wooden door.
There was no handle. Leo held up the clock.
Click.
The door opened.
Inside was a room filled with hundreds of photographs.
People.
Places.
Moments.

But every photograph had something missing.
A birthday cake with no candles.
A school with no students.
A family with one empty chair.
Then Leo saw a photograph of his own grandfather. Beside it was a note: “Some moments were stolen. Someone must return them.” Suddenly, the silver clock began ticking. For the first time, it showed a real time. 12:00.
Leo placed the clock against the wall. A bright flash filled the room.
The photographs began changing.
The missing candles appeared.
The empty chair was filled.
The forgotten moments returned.
Then Leo saw one final photograph. It showed the railway station. And himself standing beside his grandfather. Leo turned around.

His grandfather was standing behind him, smiling.
“I wondered when you'd find it,” he said.
Leo stared at the clock. “Why did it stop?” His grandfather laughed softly.
“Because it wasn't broken.” He took the clock from Leo's hand.
“It was waiting.” Outside, the town clocks suddenly began ticking again.
And from that day forward, the silver clock always showed the correct time. Except at midnight. At midnight, it showed a different moment from Leo's future.
