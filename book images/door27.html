Behind Door 27

Author: Nora Vale

Behind Door 27 is a mysterious horror story about Daniel, who discovers a strange door that suddenly appears in a familiar hotel hallway. Behind it, he finds a room filled with clocks and a warning that could change everything. As midnight approaches, Daniel realizes that some doors are better left unopened.
