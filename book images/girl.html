The Girl Who Collected Stars

author:Clara Wren

A magical story about Elia, a twelve-year-old girl who collects fallen stars in a tiny glass jar. When one star mysteriously disappears from the night sky, Elia follows a trail of sparks into a hidden forest tower and discovers a secret that could change the sky forever.
