The Day My Alarm Clock Quit

Clara Wren

                                            A Very Serious Morning Disaster By Clara Wren

At exactly 7:00 a.m., my alarm rang.

I opened one eye.

“Five more minutes,” I whispered.

At 7:05, it rang again.
I slapped the snooze button.
At 7:10, it rang again. This time, I threw my pillow at it. Silence.“Finally,” I smiled.
Then my alarm clock spoke. “Fine. Sleep forever.”
I sat straight up.
“WAIT… WHAT?!”

The clock flashed 7:11. “You heard me,” it said. “I quit.”
I stared at it. “You’re an alarm clock. You don’t get to quit.”
“I’ve been waking you up for three years,”
it replied. “You ignore me every morning. I have feelings too.”

I was too shocked to answer. Then my phone started ringing. “Don’t even think about it,” the alarm clock warned.
I picked up my phone. “Good morning!” my phone said cheerfully. “DON’T ENCOURAGE HER,” the alarm clock shouted.
My phone immediately went silent.
I jumped out of bed.
“Okay! I’m awake!”
“Too late,” said the clock. I looked at the time.
8:02 a.m.
My school bus left at 8:00.
I stared at the clock.
The clock stared back.
Then it smiled. “Have a nice day.”

I grabbed my bag and ran outside wearing one shoe.

And that was the day I learned an important lesson:
Never argue with an alarm clock. Especially when it knows your snooze button history.
