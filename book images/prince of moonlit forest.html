The Prince And The Witch Of The Moonlit Forest

Isla Bennett

                                            Some secrets are hidden for a reason. By Isla Bennett

Long ago, beyond seven silver mountains, there stood a kingdom called Eldoria.
The kingdom was peaceful until one night, every star disappeared from the sky. The king summoned his wisest scholars, but none could explain what had happened. Then an old traveler arrived at the palace and whispered of a witch who lived deep inside the forbidden Moonlit Forest.

“She knows where the stars have gone,” he said. “But no one who enters her forest ever returns the same.”

The prince, Adrian, refused to wait. Carrying only a lantern and his father’s old compass, he rode into the forest before sunrise.

The trees grew taller with every step. Their silver leaves whispered above him, and strange blue flowers opened wherever his horse walked. At midnight, Adrian saw a small cottage glowing between the trees.An old witch stood outside.
“You shouldn't have come here,”

she said.
“Where are the stars?” Adrian asked. The witch looked toward the empty sky. “They were not stolen,” she replied. “They were hidden.” She led him into her cottage and opened an ancient wooden box. Inside was a tiny piece of glowing crystal.
“This is the Heart of the Sky,”
she explained. “Long ago, your kingdom's rulers used its magic to make Eldoria prosperous. But every year, they took more magic from it. Tonight, the Heart finally broke.”
Adrian stared at the crystal. “Can it be repaired?” “Yes,” said the witch. “But only someone willing to give up something precious can restore it.”

Adrian thought of his crown, his treasures, and his palace. Then he remembered the people of Eldoria. He removed the royal golden ring from his finger—the symbol of his future crown.

“Take this.”
The witch smiled.
“You finally understand.”

She placed the ring beside the crystal. A brilliant light filled the cottage. Outside, the forest began to glow. One by one, the missing stars returned to the sky.

When Adrian returned to Eldoria, he told the king everything. From that day forward, the kingdom protected the Heart of the Sky instead of using its magic.

And deep within the Moonlit Forest, the witch continued to watch over the stars. Sometimes, when the moon was full, Adrian would look toward the forest and see a tiny golden light shining between the trees. He always smiled. Because he knew that some of the greatest treasures in the world were not meant to be owned. They were meant to be protected.
