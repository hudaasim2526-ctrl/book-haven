The House That Whispered At Night

Author: Isla Bennett

The House That Whispered at Midnight is a chilling mystery about curiosity, secrets, and a house that should have been left alone. When Maya begins hearing strange whispers coming from an abandoned house at midnight, she decides to discover the truth. But some mysteries are waiting to be found—and some doors are never meant to be opened. As Maya steps inside, she soon discovers that the house may know much more about her than she ever imagined.
