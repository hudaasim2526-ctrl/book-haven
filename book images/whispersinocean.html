Whispers Beneath The Ocean

Amelia Grace

                                            The Sea Was Hiding a Secret By Amelia Grace

The first time Nora heard the whisper, she was alone on the beach.
“Come closer…” She looked around. Nobody was there.

Only the waves rolled quietly across the shore. Nora stepped into the water.
The whisper came again. “Find the blue stone.”

The next morning, she discovered a strange blue stone beneath the sand. It was smooth, cold, and glowing faintly.
The moment she picked it up, the ocean went completely still.
Then a wave rushed toward her feet and left behind a tiny golden compass.

Its needle pointed straight out to sea. Nora couldn't resist.
She borrowed her brother's little boat and followed the compass beyond the familiar waters.
After an hour, the compass began spinning wildly.
Then the sea beneath her turned bright blue. A shape appeared underwater.
An enormous stone staircase. Nora stared. There was no way it could be there. She took a deep breath and dove beneath the surface. At the bottom of the stairs stood a magnificent underwater gate covered in shells and glowing symbols. The blue stone in her hand suddenly floated forward.
Click. The gate opened.

Inside was a hidden chamber filled with hundreds of glass bottles.
Each bottle contained a tiny glowing light. Nora touched one.
A whisper came from inside. “Thank you.”

Suddenly, every bottle began glowing brighter. A beautiful underwater city appeared beyond the chamber—its towers sparkling beneath the waves.

A friendly voice whispered: “Long ago, humans forgot this place. The sea remembered.” Nora smiled.
She placed the blue stone on a small pedestal. The city lit up completely.

When Nora returned to the surface, the compass had disappeared. So had the stone.
But the next morning, she found a tiny seashell beside her bed.
When she held it to her ear, she heard the ocean whisper: “Keep our secret.” Nora smiled.
She had discovered a world beneath the waves. And she knew she would never look at the sea the same way again.
