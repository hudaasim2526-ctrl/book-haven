The Day My Alarm Clock Quit

author:Clara Wren

One ordinary morning turns into a hilarious disaster when a stubborn alarm clock decides it has had enough. After years of being ignored, the alarm finally decides to quit its job. But without its usual morning warnings, everything quickly goes wrong. With a missed school bus and only one shoe, this morning becomes unforgettable. Sometimes, even an alarm clock deserves to be listened to!
