The Last Winter Letter

Author: Evelyn Hart

When Maya discovers a mysterious letter beneath her door on the coldest morning of winter, she is shocked to see the name of Elias—the childhood friend who disappeared eight years ago. The letter contains a warning, a date from the future, and clues that lead Maya back to the old train station where they were last seen together. As she uncovers hidden messages and forgotten secrets, Maya realizes that Elias may have survived after all. But with one final train arriving at exactly 6:10, she must uncover the truth before the last letter reaches its destination.
