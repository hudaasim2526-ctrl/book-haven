The Moonlight Garden

By Elara Vane

                                            A Tale of Secrets Beneath the Silver Sky By Elara Vane

The garden had been locked for twenty years. No one in the village knew why.
One quiet evening, seventeen-year-old Clara found an old silver key inside her grandmother’s desk. A tiny moon was carved into its handle.
That night, at exactly midnight, she followed a strange trail of silver light into the woods. It led to the forgotten garden.

The moment Clara unlocked the rusty gate, every flower inside began to glow. She stepped forward. Then she heard it Ding.

A small golden bell hanging from a tree had moved by itself. Clara looked around. Nothing.
She followed the sound deeper into the garden until she discovered a stone fountain. In its center was a little glass box. Inside was a single glowing seed.
A note lay beneath it: Plant this before the moon disappears.” Clara looked up. The moon was already fading. She quickly dug a small hole and planted the seed.
For a moment, nothing happened. Then— WHOOSH!
The entire garden burst into silver light. The trees shimmered, the flowers opened, and thousands of tiny glowing butterflies filled the sky.
But Clara noticed something else. At the end of the garden stood a door that had never been there before. She slowly approached it. Before she could touch the handle, the door opened.
Behind it was her grandmother, smiling. Clara froze. Her grandmother had passed away years ago. But instead of looking frightened, Clara smiled.
On the floor between them was another silver key. Her grandmother pointed toward it and whispered,
"Now the garden is yours"
The door closed.
The moon returned to its full brightness. And from that night on, whenever the village saw silver lights glowing in the woods, they knew the Moonlight Garden had chosen its new keeper.
