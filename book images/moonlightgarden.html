The Moonlight Garden

Author: Sarah Williams

For twenty years, the Moonlight Garden has remained locked, hiding a secret no one in the village could explain. But when Clara discovers a mysterious silver key in her grandmother’s desk, she is drawn into the forgotten garden under the light of a fading moon. Glowing flowers, a magical seed, and a mysterious door lead her to a discovery that will change her life forever. Will Clara uncover the garden’s secret before the moon disappears?”** 🌙✨
